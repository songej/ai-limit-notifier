Know when your AI limits reset.

See ChatGPT Work/Codex and Claude usage in your toolbar, get notified when a limit resets, and continue where you left off.

Get it on the Chrome Web StoreChrome extension

At a glance

  • Toolbar badge: see current usage, or the time left while a confirmed limit is active.
  • Popup: see the relevant limits for each enabled service, with reset times and remaining waits.
  • Optional alerts: get a reset notification, a 90% warning, or model-limit alerts when those options are enabled.
  • Continue conversation: after a reset, return to the recent conversation and send a continue message only after you click the button.

Supported limits

  • ChatGPT: the allowance shared by ChatGPT Work and Codex. Regular chat message caps are not included.
  • Claude: the session and weekly limits shared with Claude Code.

Privacy by design

No separate sign-up, analytics, advertising, or developer-operated backend. Usage data stays in your browser, and the extension does not read conversation history. Continue conversation runs only after you click it. Read the privacy policy

FAQ

When does my Claude limit reset?

The popup shows the reset time reported for each available limit, including the session and weekly limits.

Does it show regular ChatGPT message limits?

No. It tracks the allowance shared by ChatGPT Work and Codex.
