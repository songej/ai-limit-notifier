Know when your AI limits reset.

See your Claude and ChatGPT usage and reset times right in Chrome.

Add to ChromeFree on the Chrome Web Store

Features

  • Toolbar badge: your usage, or the time left while you are at a limit.
  • Reset notification: when a limit resets, click Continue conversation to pick up where you left off.
  • Optional: a warning at 90% of a limit, and per-model limit tracking.

Supported limits

  • Claude: the session and weekly limits shared with Claude Code.
  • ChatGPT: the limit shared by Codex and ChatGPT Work. Regular chat limits are not included.

Privacy

Usage data stays on your device. No separate sign-up, ads or developer server, and conversation history is not read. Read the privacy policy
